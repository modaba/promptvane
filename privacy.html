Privacy Policy - Prompt Vane Agent

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Prompt Vane Agent is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Prompt Vane to upload that channel's own videos and read that channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube Data API: uploading videos, setting thumbnails and metadata, and reading the channel's videos and public comments.
  • YouTube Analytics API: reading aggregate channel statistics (views, watch time, subscribers).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to produce performance reports for the channel owner. It is stored locally on the owner's computer, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties. Comments are read only in aggregate to understand which topics viewers want; no personal profiles of viewers are built.

Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for up to 24 months. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the app then deletes its stored tokens.

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
